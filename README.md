# 👥 Customer Segmentation using RFM Analysis and K-Means

An end-to-end Data Science project that segments e-commerce customers based on their purchasing behavior using **RFM Analysis**, **K-Means Clustering**, and **PCA**.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, model evaluation, and an interactive **Streamlit dashboard**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding customer behavior is important for businesses because different customers have different purchasing patterns.

This project analyzes an online retail dataset and groups customers into meaningful segments based on:

- **Recency** — How recently a customer purchased
- **Frequency** — How often a customer purchased
- **Monetary** — How much a customer spent

The customer segments can help businesses design targeted marketing strategies and improve customer retention.

📊 RFM Analysis

Three customer-level metrics were calculated.

Recency

Number of days since the customer's most recent purchase.

Lower Recency generally indicates a more recently active customer.

Frequency

Number of unique invoices/orders made by the customer.

Higher Frequency indicates more frequent purchasing behavior.

Monetary

Total revenue generated by the customer.

Higher Monetary value indicates greater customer spending.

🤖 Customer Segmentation

K-Means clustering was used to group customers based on their RFM characteristics.

The project identified 2 customer segments.

🟢 High-Value Loyal Customers

Characteristics:

Average Recency: ~51 days
Average Frequency: ~12.59 purchases
Average Monetary Value: ~£6,619
Customers: 2,352
Revenue contribution: ~£15.57M

These customers purchase frequently, have purchased recently, and generate high revenue.

🟠 At-Risk / Low-Engagement Customers

Characteristics:

Average Recency: ~302 days
Average Frequency: ~2.09 purchases
Average Monetary Value: ~£617
Customers: 3,526
Revenue contribution: ~£2.17M

These customers have not purchased recently and show relatively low purchasing frequency and spending.

📈 Model Evaluation

The number of clusters was evaluated using:

Elbow Method

The Elbow Method examines the inertia of K-Means models across different numbers of clusters.

Silhouette Score

The Silhouette Score measures how well-separated the resulting clusters are.

The final K-Means model was selected based on the clustering evaluation results.

🧭 PCA Visualization

PCA (Principal Component Analysis) was used to reduce the RFM feature space to two dimensions.

This allows the customer clusters to be visualized on a 2D chart.
